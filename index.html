Top fields for the next 7 days

Week score: each day scores 0–100 from fresh snow (including the day before), wind against the field's hold threshold, rain risk at the base, storm visibility, warmth and sunshine, adjusted by the mountain's rating of the wind direction. The week score is half the average day and half the average of the best three days. Mini strip: weekday, weather, consensus snow (cm), day score bar.

    Things to watch

      Snow cams are linked to each field's own webcam page rather than embedded, because the fields own those images and the URLs change without notice.

      Forecast data from Open-Meteo (free, non-commercial). Global models are 10–25 km grids, so terrain is smoothed and snow totals at a specific field are estimates. Freezing level and summit wind are derived from the 850 and 700 hPa levels so every model is treated the same way. Model accuracy is scored against ERA5 reanalysis, not on-mountain observations.

    Things to watch

      This guide is written from general knowledge of each field and is meant to be corrected from local experience. Edit js/mountains.js.

      Forecast data from Open-Meteo (free, non-commercial). Global models are 10–25 km grids, so terrain is smoothed and snow totals at a specific field are estimates. Freezing level and summit wind are derived from the 850 and 700 hPa levels so every model is treated the same way. Model accuracy is scored against ERA5 reanalysis, not on-mountain observations. Always check the field's own snow report and road status before driving.
